# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Project participants must communicate respectfully and constructively. Examples
of expected behavior include:

- welcoming people of different backgrounds and experience levels;
- giving and receiving technical feedback in good faith;
- focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people; and
- respecting privacy, attribution, and project history.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider context,
severity, and whether harmful behavior is repeated.

To report conduct privately, use a private contact method listed by the
[maintainer](https://github.com/tuunanet). If none is available, open a minimal
issue asking to establish private contact without including sensitive details.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
